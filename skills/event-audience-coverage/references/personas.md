# Attendee persona taxonomy (v1)

This taxonomy is a starting point, not an exclusive identity system. A participant may fit multiple groups. Do not infer individual demographic attributes. For each page, decide whether a group is primary, secondary, incidental or not applicable.

## Independent audience lenses
Do not collapse motivations into job roles:
- **Experience:** nontechnical, new to Drupal, exploring, proficient, advanced, unrelated to Drupal.
- **Intent:** learn, evaluate/buy, sell/partner, build, contribute, hire, research, network, showcase.
- **Sector:** enterprise, startup/SaaS, government, education, nonprofit/social impact, agency, community.
- **Event days (DrupalAsia 2027):** Day 1 (Jan 28) AI Summit / Imagine; Day 2 (Jan 29) Conference / Discover; Day 3 (Jan 30) Contribution Sprints + Hackathon / Build.

## Practical grouping
For dashboards, optionally group into: Learners; Drupal and OSS practitioners; Design and content; Buyers and adopters; Sellers and partners; SaaS and product; AI and research; Public sector, education and social impact; Infrastructure and security; Ecosystem and community. Preserve detailed rows in underlying data.
