# How to adapt the audit to different Drupal gatherings

The same persona taxonomy should be **filtered by event purpose**, not mechanically applied to every event.

| Gathering | Primary audit emphasis | Example audiences to consider | Common `N/A` cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DrupalCon | Wide community and market representation | Practitioners, adopters, agencies, buyers, contributors, learners | None by default; justify at page level |
| DrupalCamp | Regional learning and community value | Developers, site builders, students, local organisations, agencies | Investors on a narrow workshop page |
| Local meetup | Clear local invitation and topic fit | First-timers, practitioners, local community | Enterprise procurement on a hands-on meetup announcement |
| Contribution day | Participation paths and onboarding | Maintainers, first-time contributors, designers, documentation authors | Sales leads on a contribution instructions page |
| Workshop / training | Prerequisites and learning outcomes | Target skill levels, learners, educators | Sponsors on an instructor handout |
| AI / DXP summit | Cross-discipline adoption and collaboration | AI teams, product teams, enterprise adopters, public sector | Deep Drupal core contribution on a policy panel page |
| Virtual gathering | Remote access and community engagement | Remote contributors, international attendees | On-site travel information for fully online sessions |

## Event profile to give the skill

```text
Event: [name]
Format: [conference/camp/meetup/workshop/contribution day/other]
Scale: [local/regional/national/international or unknown]
Audience promise: [purpose, topics, outcomes]
Participation: [in person/online/hybrid]
Pages to audit: [URLs or pasted content]
Relevant constraints: [languages, beginner support, cost, accessibility, etc.]
```

## Rule of interpretation

A missing persona is an editorial gap **only when the page or wider event is intended to serve that persona**. Explain primary, secondary, incidental and `N/A` audiences before making recommendations. One person may occupy multiple personas.
