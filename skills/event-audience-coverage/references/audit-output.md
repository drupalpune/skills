# Audit output contract

Always include:
1. Scope and evidence: URL(s), fetched/inspected count, pages skipped, retrieval limitations, audit date if known.
2. Short executive summary: strongest coverage, largest gaps, intended audience balance.
3. Persona coverage matrix: either page rows × personas columns for site or persona rows for one page, with legend 0–4/N/A/? and evidence links.
4. Top gaps with short rationale and exact on-page/URL references.
5. Recommended edits: page/section, audience, proposed copy or structure, priority, effort and reason.
6. Confidence/limitations and notes on areas not evaluated.

For large sites, present a concise grouped matrix plus detailed downloadable CSV when possible. CSV must preserve detailed persona IDs, scores, evidence and confidence.

For repeat runs: baseline identifier/date, page scope differences, improvement/regression notes, and unchanged gaps. Only compare verifiable like-for-like records.
